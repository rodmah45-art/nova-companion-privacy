Privacy Policy

🔒 Nova Companion does not collect, sell, or share your personal data with any third party. Your conversations stay on your own private server.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Nova Companion ("the App"), developed by KingsDev Studio ("we", "us"), handles your information. By using Nova Companion, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Effective date: October 10, 2026

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Nova Companion does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Contacts or call logs
  • Any analytics or usage tracking data
  • Crash reports sent to our servers

3. How the App Works

Nova Companion is a self-hosted AI companion. All conversation data is sent directly between your device and your own private server — not to KingsDev Studio's servers.

  • Voice input: Processed locally on your device using Vosk offline speech recognition. Audio is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Chat messages: Sent to your configured server URL using HTTPS. The AI response is generated by the DeepSeek API as configured by you.
  • Conversation history: Stored on your own server's database. KingsDev Studio has no access to this data.

4. Permissions Used

Permission Why it's needed
Microphone To listen to your voice commands for offline speech recognition. Audio is processed on-device only — never uploaded.
Notification access Optional. To read incoming notification content aloud (e.g. "You have a message from..."). Notifications are not stored or sent anywhere.
Display over other apps To show the floating chat bubble over other apps. No data is collected through this.
Bluetooth To allow Nova to toggle Bluetooth on/off when you ask. No Bluetooth data is collected.
Camera To control the flashlight only. The camera is never activated for photos or video.
Notifications (POST) To show service notifications while the microphone or floating bubble is active.
Internet To communicate with your configured private AI server.
Set Alarm / Schedule Alarm To set alarms and timers when you ask Nova.

5. Third-Party Services

The App communicates with the following third-party service based on your own configuration:

  • DeepSeek API — AI language model provider. Your messages are sent to DeepSeek's servers for AI response generation. Please review DeepSeek's Privacy Policy.
  • Vosk — Offline speech recognition library. Runs entirely on your device. No data is sent to Vosk servers.

6. Data Security

All communication between the app and your server uses HTTPS encryption. We recommend keeping your server token secure and not sharing it with others.

7. Children's Privacy

Nova Companion is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.
